solo7-theme — kitchen sink

Every token and component, under all 5 imprint accents, in both color schemes. Left column is forced light, right is forced dark (via data-scheme) — independent of your OS setting, so both are always visible for regression diffing. Contrast ratios shown next to accent swatches are the same numbers make check gates on.

Swatch ratios are foreground-on-bg. AA body ≥ 4.5:1 · AA large/UI ≥ 3:1.
